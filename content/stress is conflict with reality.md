from p14 of exurb1a's [[Poems for the Lost Because I'm Lost Too]]

When something isn't the way we want it to be that is the source of stress. This also applies to our self-image, if we don't exercise as much or aren't as happy as we want this creates more stress

This along with the fact that depressed people make the "easier" choices that make them even more depressed is probably the reason they enter the downward spiral of mental health

We should be aware of this so that we don't put much value on being someone we are not and creating unnecessary stress
