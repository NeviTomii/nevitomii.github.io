There is only so much motivation I can have before exhausting myself. I should prioritize actions that:

1. do the most good
2. help me
3. are easy (leave more motivation for other things)
4. are fun

By my own moral standards, it would be better if I were vegan, but the difficulty, denial of fun and effort spent outweigh the amount of good I could achieve.

Still, I would support any legislation moving all of society towards veganism. the amount of good caused by everyone being vegan flips around and is suddenly a lot more important than my enjoyment of meat
