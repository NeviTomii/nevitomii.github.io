best way to help the world is to work towards one world government.

best way to do that without money is to contribute to international systems, so that escaping out of those systems is too costly and the benefit too large, granting credibility to international authority

this can be done through protocols for: internet, financial transactions, logistics, ai safety, identity/surveillence systems, disaster coordinations channels etc

or, to prove supranational coordination is effective, prepare decentralized packages for nations to follow during repeating stress events: pandemic, financial collapse, extreme weather events,

examples:

Machine-readable data schemas (JSON Schema, FHIR-like models, XBRL extensions).
Legal templates for emergency mutual recognition or temporary authority transfer.
Operational playbooks with decision trees, RACI matrices, and escalation paths.
Evaluation frameworks that score national or institutional responses against measurable criteria.

publicly hosted, correctly licensed, send to relevant bodies
