It is interesting how an Innocuous statement such as ["May all being be happy"](https://www.kezjsouvsechnybytostistastny.cz/) is such a magnet for naive people

I think it would be beneficial to us all if everyone agreeing with this just took a reality check. Do I really want _**everyone**_ to be happy? No wealthy person, politician or coworker I wish bad things upon?

realizing that even nice sounding statements may not be true is a good first step towards critical thinking of what I read online
