The rich get richer and the poor get ... richer faster?

from an article by [Tibor Rutar](https://statsandsociety.substack.com/p/how-does-pinkers-enlightenment-now): "the 1980s and 1990s – did see an increase, perhaps even a sharp increase, in inequality, ... However, inequality then stopped increasing in the early 2000s, and has since stabilized or declined."
![[wealth inequality is going down.png|580]]

This is crazy!

I don't understand how we can be so hostile towards rich, privileged or powerful  when, ever since the creation of social hierarchies, the direct power elites hold has declined enormously. There have been many reversals, but in terms of legal, political, social and income equality, people living in modern democracies are extraordinarily equal by historical standards.

I was under the assumption that people are outraged because income (and by extension power) inequality began climbing again, but that doesn't seem to be the case at all! It is true that wealth inequality is rising, more money makes more money after all, but that doesn't by itself say anything about the quality of life for vast majority of people. It is better to attack what actually becomes the problem with rising wealth - political influence, inheritance, unequal opportunity etc. Those can be dealt with separately from wealth.
