In the news and on social media we see mostly what is going wrong in the world. It's in our nature to focus on what needs fixing. However, what we don't realize is how much of what is happening is actually good.

in the modern world we have so many luxuries and comforts that we take for granted. Even if there are still wars and unpopular political regimes, we made ourselves a utopia compared to living standards just few decades earlier.

seems bullish to me
