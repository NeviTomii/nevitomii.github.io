In a world where everyone lived as a hermit and cared only about their own affairs, I wouldn't be able to build myself a house. In an agricultural village, if I had strong enough connections, I could ask people I knew to help me build one—but I would still be building it myself in a meaningful sense, and I would be expected to return the favor by helping them build theirs.

What is genuinely awesome (in the original sense of the word) is that today I don't even need to earn the median income to pay someone else to build a house for me. Our society is so extraordinarily wealthy beyond anything what people from a few hundred years ago might imagine and we take it for granted.

Even with real estate prices as high as they are today, we should really be thankful for all we have
