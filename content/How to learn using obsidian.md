It is important to write down information, so we don't forget.  [[We learn by remembering]]
There are so many inputs in our lives, and most are forgotten, even those we deem important.

How to write things down:

1) Capture
2) Process
3) write

# 1) Capture

 writing down what I hear
highlighting and quoting what i read

**Fleeting notes**
	notes just to remember something, then discarded
	highlights from inner monologue

**Reference notes**
	quotes and ideas from articles, videos, podcasts, books
	make sure to mark down page number, few word summary
	put them into [[Inbox]], later process into proper references and atomic notes

knowledge gathering != understanding
it is not enough to capture fleeting thoughts, we need to process them

# 2) Process

transform captured thoughts into atomic notes

**Atomic notes**
	high cohesion, low coupling
	= work well together, don't need one to understand another
	should be as simple as possible
	=teachable unit of knowledge

**how to create an atomic note:**
title - enough to know what it is in a week
body - enough to know what it is in a few years
tags - atomic tag, topic tag
links to as many other atomic notes in body
references - links outside of obsidian
use a property called up with a link to one related atomic note

what i want to finish with should be a composition of atomic notes

**how to process fleeting notes:**
review a fleeting note using spaced repetition plugin
if it takes less than 5 minutes do it now and delete
otherwise do now or set difficulty and save for later
do now can mean:
	cut and paste into different note (buy something)
	
když píšu atomic note i need to ask myself how that idea can be connected to other notes

interrogate the content of your ideas.
read the subtext.
stretch their relevancy
flip them over and turn them inside out.
page 69 zetrelkasten book

for example: connectikn between soil erosion and intimacy erosion or connection between right to refuse gift and right to refuse to buy something from the lens of powersynamics

dont forget to explain why links are made

when sourcing something, link to a literature note that has keyworda about what i am linking, and from the literature note link outside to the internet or book

# 3) Write

compose clusters of atomic notes into finished notes - essay, exam revision, idea

dont forget to ask AI to support my writing using quotes from people
