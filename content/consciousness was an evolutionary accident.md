in the evolutionary arms race between a predator and prey, you win by predicting your opponent accurately.

for that you need memory and inteligence; a world model you can extrapolate into the future

over time this pressure to evolve created a world model so good it mapped (with some huge caveats) onto reality. Humans can clearly "perceive" everything around them, imagine the past and the future, connect thought with action. This at some point sparked self-awareness

human consciousness wouldn't exist (or at least begin as quickly) without the predator and pray relationship
